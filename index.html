Common questions

Short answers — see Support for the full guide.

Does CrispVoice send my audio to a server?

No. Recording, pause analysis, transcription, and rendering all run on your iPhone. CrispVoice has no backend.

Which iPhones does it run on?

iPhone 15 or newer running iOS 18 or later. CrispVoice depends on Apple Intelligence's on-device Speech Analyzer for accurate transcription, which is only available on the A17 Pro and later silicon — that's iPhone 15 and the entire iPhone 16 / 17 line. Older iPhones aren't supported.

What happens to my Snip & Cut edits if I close the app?

Every edit is auto-saved to a draft on the project. Reopening the project drops you back exactly where you left off.

How do I cancel Pro?

Open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → CrispVoice → Cancel. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period; you keep Pro until then.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com within Apple's refund window and select the CrispVoice purchase.

Where do I report a bug?

Email support@crispvoice.app. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description — we usually reply within one business day.
